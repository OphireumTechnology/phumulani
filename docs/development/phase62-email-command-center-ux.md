# Phase 62: Email Command Center Staff UX & Simplification Guide

## 1. Overview & Human-Centered Design
The redesigned Email Command Center (`/admin/master/email`) is tailored for non-technical frontline staff, caseworkers, and directors. It transitions away from raw JSON/diagnostics into an intuitive, high-velocity institutional communication workspace while retaining advanced telemetry behind collapsible accordions.

## 2. Core Functional Views
- **Inbox:** All unread and active correspondence across assigned mailboxes.
- **Assigned to Me:** Filtered view displaying only correspondence assigned to the current caseworker.
- **Drafts:** AI-assisted and staff-authored messages in editable draft status.
- **Awaiting Approval:** Centralized approval queue for Executive Director Comfort Dondo and Safeguarding Lead Sister Miriam K.
- **Sent:** Historical log of human-approved and provider-acknowledged transmissions.
- **Follow-ups:** Scheduled touchpoints and CRM-linked donor/grant reminders.
- **Search & Filter:** Instant full-text search with category, department, and priority filters.

## 3. Quad-Action Approval Controls
In the "Awaiting Approval" interface, reviewers have four clear actions:
1. **Approve:** Transmit message verbatim through provider connector.
2. **Edit & Approve:** Modify subject, body, or recipients, recomputing content hash and dispatching.
3. **Reject:** Return draft to author with feedback notes; prevents transmission.
4. **Escalate / Return:** Elevate to Executive Directorate or Safeguarding Lead for sensitive matters.

## 4. Mobile & Tablet Adaptations
- Single-column responsive layout for mobile caseworkers on outreach visits.
- Large touch targets for approval and quick-reply buttons.
- Sticky safe exit and urgent crisis escalation triggers available in mobile viewports.
