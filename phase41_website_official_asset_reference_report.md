## 4. Authentic vs. Illustrative Boundary Governance

A critical directive of Phase 41 is maintaining the uncompromising boundary between authentic organizational assets and illustrative/decorative media:

1. **Founder Representation:**
   - **Rule:** Under NO circumstances may an AI model generate a fictional photographic portrait and attribute it to real founder Comfort Dondo.
   - **Implementation:** When official high-resolution photography is not directly bundled, the platform displays the dignified authorized monogram avatar (`CD`) or authentic vector silhouette.
2. **Community & Survivor Gatherings:**
   - **Rule:** AI-generated imagery must NEVER be presented as real documentary photography of actual confidential Phumulani events or survivor circles.
   - **Safeguarding Compliance:** Under VAWA Section 1367 and humanitarian best practices, survivor confidentiality is absolute. All community scenes are explicitly curated as stylized vector art communicating warmth, sisterhood, and cultural sanctuary without depicting identifiable individuals.
3. **Program & Service Metaphors:**
   - Vector illustrations represent services (housing, tea circles, mentorship, legal advocacy) using cultural geometric motifs, warm tones, and welcoming symbolism.
