## 1. Capacity Measurement Standard & Anti-Inflation Rule

Program capacity utilization is audited against physical classroom, clinician cohort, and mentor ratios. In accordance with Section 16 of the master directive, **waitlisted individuals are explicitly excluded from the numerator** to prevent artificial over-utilization inflation:

$$\text{Utilization \%} = \frac{\text{Confirmed Active Enrollments}}{\text{Total Operational Cohort Capacity}} \times 100$$
