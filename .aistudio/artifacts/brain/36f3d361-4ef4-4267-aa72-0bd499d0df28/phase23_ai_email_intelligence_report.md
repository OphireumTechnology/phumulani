## 2. MULTI-ENTITY CONSTITUENT EXTRACTION ENGINE

The entity extraction engine processes incoming message bodies and structured headers to identify:

1. **Constituent Identity:** Resolves sender full name, organizational affiliation, and verified email address against the constituent database.
2. **Financial Values:** Parses monetary pledges, major donor gifts (e.g., "$50,000 challenge grant", "$500 recurring"), tax receipt IDs, and transaction dates.
3. **Grant Metadata:** Identifies funder identities (e.g., "Bush Foundation", "Minnesota Department of Human Services"), RFP numbers, LOI invitation codes, and submission deadlines.
4. **Corporate CSR Details:** Identifies sponsoring entities, employee volunteer counts, workplace giving matching ratios, and event tiers.
5. **Safeguarding Crisis Indicators:** Scans for keywords indicating domestic danger, emergency transitional shelter needs, or confidential survivor services to trigger immediate fail-closed quarantine.

## 3. NINE APPROVED RESPONSE TONE STYLES

AI draft generation supports 9 distinct tone styles:

1. **`professional`:** Balanced, courteous, structured organizational voice.
2. **`formal`:** Respectful, legally grounded, institutional voice suitable for board, government, and audit inquiries.
3. **`warm_welcoming`:** Empathetic, culturally affirming sisterhood tone for community members, volunteers, and grassroots supporters.
4. **`executive`:** Strategic, concise, leadership perspective for Executive Director Comfort Dondo.
5. **`donor_appreciation`:** Deeply grateful, mission-centered, highlighting concrete community impact and 501(c)(3) compliance.
6. **`partnership_development`:** Collaborative, value-aligned tone for institutional and community alliance builders.
7. **`grant_correspondence`:** Rigorous, compliance-oriented, program-specific tone meeting funder guidelines.
8. **`volunteer_coordination`:** Encouraging, clear, logistics-oriented tone for volunteer onboarding and event coordination.
9. **`community_engagement`:** Grassroots, culturally rooted voice welcoming families to Phumulani community gatherings.

## 4. SIX AI REWRITE TRANSFORMATIONS

Staff reviewers can iteratively transform any draft with one-click actions:

- **`shorten`:** Eliminates extraneous pleasantries, condenses the message to core action items while maintaining courtesy.
- **`expand`:** Elaborates on Phumulani's mission, community impact metrics, and provides detailed context on safe housing and sisterhood programs.
- **`formal`:** Replaces informal colloquialisms with precise institutional phrasing suitable for grants, auditors, and legal representatives.
- **`warm`:** Injects culturally centered warmth, sisterhood empathy, and gratitude reflecting Phumulani's African-centered community roots.
- **`executive`:** Formats the message as a high-level briefing from the Executive Director, focusing on strategic vision and definitive next steps.
- **`clarity`:** Streamlines run-on sentences, clarifies dates, locations, and deadlines, ensuring zero ambiguity for the recipient.
