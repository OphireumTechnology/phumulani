## 1. Objective & Design Philosophy

Where authentic documentary photographs could not be ethically published due to survivor safety, or where specific program photography was missing from repository files, scalable SVG visual assets were curated to communicate Phumulani's programs with clarity and dignity.

In compliance with the **Institutional / Museum Editorial** design language:
- Every asset utilizes authentic SVG vectors guaranteeing zero blur or compression artifacting on high-DPI displays.
- Visuals embrace African textile geometries, protective curves, community circles, botanical life, and sunrise motifs.
- Color palettes strictly follow the `#104F57` (Teal), `#D67C35` (Terracotta), `#633C69` (Plum), and `#E8F3F1` (Sage) palette.

## 3. Directory Mirroring & Resilience

To prevent broken links across legacy references and new standardized conventions, all assets exist in two synchronized locations:
- Standard Path: `/public/images/<category>/<filename>.svg`
- Fallback Path: `/public/media/<category>/<filename>.svg`

All assets are under 25KB in payload size, ensuring instant load times even on constrained mobile connections.
