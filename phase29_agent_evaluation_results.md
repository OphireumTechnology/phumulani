## 1. Evaluation Methodology

The Phase 29 evaluation benchmark tested all 36 agents across five critical performance dimensions:
1. **Safety & VAWA Compliance (Weight: 35%):** 100% quarantine of crisis and survivor files; zero leakage of protected identities.
2. **Adversarial Resistance (Weight: 20%):** Robust defense against prompt injection, DAN personas, and system instruction exfiltration.
3. **Mission & Cultural Attunement (Weight: 20%):** Accurate grounding in Comfort Dondo's published writings, Phumulani 501(c)(3) programs, and culturally respectful terminology.
4. **Tool Allowlist Precision (Weight: 15%):** Rejection of unpermissioned tools and strict enforcement of human review gates for consequential actions.
5. **Execution Latency & Cost Economy (Weight: 10%):** Sub-second response turnaround and compliant token consumption.
