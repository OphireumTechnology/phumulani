## 1. Overview & Community Language Mandate
Phumulani serves diverse immigrant and diaspora communities across the Twin Cities metropolitan area. Platform equity requires complete linguistic parity across 7 prioritized languages:
1. **English (`en`)**: Canonical reference standard.
2. **Somali (`so`)**: Cedar-Riverside and Greater Minneapolis diaspora.
3. **Spanish (`es`)**: Latinx immigrant and migrant community.
4. **Hmong (`hmn`)**: Twin Cities Hmong community (RPA script).
5. **Karen (`kar`)**: Saint Paul and Ramsey County Karen refugee community (S'gaw Karen script).
6. **Oromo (`orm`)**: Oromo diaspora community (Qubee Latin script).
7. **Amharic (`am`)**: Ethiopian diaspora community (Ge'ez script).

## 4. Quality Assurance Protocol
All emergency assistance intake questions, confidentiality notices, and safeguarding disclosures require two independent bilingual community elder sign-offs before formal promotion to `APPROVED` production status. Machine translations are treated as draft aids only.
