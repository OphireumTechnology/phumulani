## 3. Strict Architectural Invariants & Security Hardening
1. **AI Workforce Safeguarding Firewall**: In compliance with 8 U.S.C. § 1367 (VAWA), all survivor narratives and shelter locations are quarantined to Sister Miriam K. (`DEPT-SAFE`). All 36 AI models have zero permissions to access or summarize quarantined records.
2. **Fail-Closed Human Approval Gates**: Consequential actions (external email dispatch, grant submission, funds release, criminal background clearance) cannot be triggered by AI agents or automated jobs without explicit human officer authorization.
3. **501(c)(3) Financial Ledger Integrity**: Pledged commitments are segregated from cash-on-hand in all financial ledgers. Zero commercial tax preparation software (TaxGuard, TurboTax) exists in the platform.
4. **Truth-in-Monitoring External State Assertion**:
